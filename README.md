# Seasons of War · Proving Grounds

Play Seasons of War (SOW) in the browser: against a trained AI, pass-and-play on one device, or online against a friend.

**Play:** https://lupin857.github.io/Seasons-of-War-Proving-Grounds/

## Playing online
1. Open the game, go to **Play online**, type your name and tap **Create a room**.
2. Tap **Copy invite** and send it to your friend. The link fills in the room code for them.
3. They type their name and tap **Join a room**. The game starts as soon as they join.

No accounts are needed. The two browsers connect directly (the free PeerJS service only introduces them). Keep the page open while you play; if either of you reloads, use **Rejoin room** on the start screen.

The AI, card numbers and card stats are built into the page, so everyone plays the same AI.
