SOW Proving Grounds

Play full games against an AI that learns. Train it by letting it play itself; it keeps whatever strategies win, and every game feeds the card win rates.

Rules this simulation uses

  • Two players, first to 3, 4 or 5 points while leading (chosen when you start a game; each length has its own AI, card stats and ratings). Ties keep playing. Game stops after 20 rounds.
  • Each shop: $3 + income. Money carries over. Display of 5 units and 5 buildings; unbought cards go sideways and come back once, then are discarded.
  • Turn order: random in round 1; afterward the loser of the last battle shops first (draw: whoever shopped second).
  • Per shop: 2 units and 1 building. Once per shop you may make one extra purchase for $1 more (not a Field). Carpenter's Guild adds extra building buys (Fields included) and makes buildings $1 cheaper (minimum $1).
  • Selling: on your shop turn you may sell any number of cards you didn't buy this shop, for $1 each. Selling doesn't use a buy or end your turn. A sold unit or building drops one level; the card goes to the bottom of its deck (Fields return to the supply, and a level from Mason's Guild returns the Guild). Selling a unit is secret: your opponent sees that you sold a unit, but not which one, and keeps seeing it in your forces.
  • Fields ($1, +$1 income) and Recruits ($1, 1-power token) are always for sale. City holds 5 buildings; duplicates share a slot, Fields don't.
  • Mobilize: up to 5 unique units (+Reserve Encampment); Mercenary and Recruits don't count. Smithy Armaments, Study Training and Barracks Recruits are placed here. Farmhand and Arsonist pay out if they're in your deck.
  • Recruits and Mercenaries are discarded after the battle phase.
  • Hidden information: the AI sees what is public (purchases, cities, money, the order you used last battle), never your current deck order.
  • Battle: same engine as the Battle Sandbox (Charge before Reveal except Pigeon Hunter, which reveals before Charges; Vanguard reveals first, deaths after abilities, damage clears when hidden). A Reveal still resolves if Recruiter covers the unit, and triggers again when it is uncovered.
  • Cathedral: +1 point while owned, but you can only win at the end of a battle phase in which you won the battle.
  • Mason's Guild attaches to one of your buildings and counts as an extra copy of it. Black Market ($3) looks at the top 3/6/9 cards of either the unit deck or the building deck (your choice, not both) once per shop; you may buy one of them for $1/$2/$3 less (by its level) (it still uses your normal buys for that kind). Unbought cards go to the bottom.
  • Not in this version: Recruitment Office and Outpost (0 copies on the sheet).
