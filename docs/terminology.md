# Terminology

Internal names (code, snapshot, saves, stats) stay as they are. `OASIS_NAMES` in `<script id="oasis-names">` maps them
to what players read. **Brian's Google Sheets are canon**: if this table, `OASIS_NAMES` and the Sheets disagree,
flag it.

## Game terms (internal → display)
SOW → Oasis · Armament(s) → Might · Recruit(s) → Drifter(s) · Field(s) → Moisture Harvester(s) ·
City → Caravan · Building(s) → Support(s) · Shield-break → Shieldbreak. Salvage/Muster → the discard pile.

## Card names
For card renames, `OASIS_NAMES` is the working list; check it against the Sheets rather than copying it here, so
there's only one list to keep up to date.

Known mismatches to confirm with Brian:
- `Studious Squire` shows as **Novice Soldier** in code; Brian chose **Observant Novice** (Nov 2026).
- `White Knight` is still mapped, but White Knight was removed as a card.

## Wording style
- "defeated", not "dies". "Might" everywhere, not Armament.
- Hand miracles: "Mobilize:/Shop: Discard this to ..."; immediate effects get a **Now** chip.
- Damaging abilities name a target that always exists (e.g. "the enemy unit ahead of me").
- Lethal scope comes from wording: "my attacks" excludes abilities; "my damage" includes them.
